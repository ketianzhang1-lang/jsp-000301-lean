## Independently established original requirement

The primary source is [Fox, Lee and Sudakov, arXiv:1107.1920v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.1920v3), introduction and Theorem 1.1 on page 2. It defines sigma as the largest order of a complete-graph subdivision and H(n) as the maximum chromatic-number/sigma ratio over n-vertex graphs. Its open question is the existence of an absolute upper constant for H(n) bounded by sqrt(n)/log(n), for all n at least 2. The preceding random-graph lower estimate is an already known motivation, not an extra unresolved half of Theorem 1.1. The paper's definition of subdivision requires internally vertex-disjoint paths. Its logarithm is natural (page 3). The present submission does not establish the paper's particular printed constant, an optimal constant, or an exact H(15); none is required by this original upper-bound question.

The primary PDF was independently retrieved through web research; erdosproblems.com/717 was unavailable during this review. The primary paper is sufficient to identify the exact uniform upper theorem. No claim is based on a failed webpage retrieval or an unrelated similar problem.
