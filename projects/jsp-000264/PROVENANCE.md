That reference explicitly states the positive-density existence question as
`erdos_318.parts.i`, and suggests the odd integers together with one even
integer as a witness. It credits Erdős, as reported by Sattler (1982), for
the observation about an infinite set with exactly one positive even element.
This project formalizes that known explicit witness. No mathematical discovery
or first-global-formalization priority is claimed.

Historical reference: R. Sattler, *On Erdős property P₁ for the sequence of
squarefree numbers*, Nederl. Akad. Wetensch. Indag. Math. (1982), 341–346.
The attribution follows the inspected Formal Conjectures statement. The
historical paper is cited for attribution; its full text was not inspected
in this work.

The P₁ definition is reproduced from the Apache-2.0 Formal Conjectures project,
copyright 2026 The Formal Conjectures Authors. Its placeholder proofs are
neither imported nor treated as evidence. All solution proofs here were
independently written with OpenAI ChatGPT / Codex assistance under the
submitting account's direction. Mathlib supplies standard arithmetic, finite
sets and limit theorems.

The complete part-i theorem is claimed as a formalization contribution; part ii
and the other P₁ variants are outside scope. The square counterexample already
proved in the consulted source was not copied or submitted as new work.
