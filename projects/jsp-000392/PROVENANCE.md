to Geoffrey Exoo, *A Lower Bound for Schur Numbers and Multicolor Ramsey Numbers
of K3*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 1 (1994), R8, pp. 1-3.

Source: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v1i1r8/pdf/

The 80 lower-half labels in seedHalf were transcribed from page 2; the upper
half follows the paper's x ↔ 161-x symmetry. The integer partition is used as
mathematical data; this package does not redistribute the article. The
reflection/tripling argument is classical Schur-number mathematics, and the
sum-free-partition-to-Ramsey-coloring reduction is described in Exoo's paper.
The formal Lean implementation and induction were independently written in
this work with OpenAI ChatGPT assistance under the submitting account's
direction. This is formalization of known mathematics, not new mathematics.
