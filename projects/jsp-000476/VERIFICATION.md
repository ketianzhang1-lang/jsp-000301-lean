## Reproduce and inspect

```sh
lake exe cache get Mathlib
bash scripts/verify.sh
bash scripts/replay_targets.sh
```

whole-import replay. Once compilation has finished, the selected-dependency
replay can also be run separately with `scripts/replay_targets.sh`; it avoids
replaying unused imported Mathlib declarations. `--resume` is optional
and accepts only matching fingerprints. NaNoda requires Rust/Cargo and builds the
locked checker dependencies. The exporter is pinned at
`8554815c2dc6b7abe99ec1f08849c9759ba77947`; NaNoda is pinned at
