## Contribution and exact scope

Submit an independently written Lean formalization of the known quadratic obstruction for JSP-000388 / Erdos 477. For all integer a, b, c with a != 0 and a dividing b, no set A of integers makes every integer uniquely representable as an A-element plus a value of a*x^2+b*x+c. This includes b=0, both signs of a, arbitrary A, and a Mathlib polynomial-evaluation endpoint. Uniqueness concerns value pairs, not polynomial inputs.

**Partial scope:** this does not prove the original general existence result, whose known answer is positive for certain higher even powers. It does not settle the cubic case or claim new mathematics or global first priority. Historical mathematical credit is retained in PROVENANCE.md.
